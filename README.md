## Free AI-Powered Tarot Readings

Sites using artificial intelligence to provide personalized, context-aware tarot interpretations.

- [Taro's Tarot](https://tarostarot.com) - AI-powered tarot readings with personalized interpretations based on your specific questions. Features 22+ spreads, dream interpretation, tarot journaling, and the ability to save reading history. 4.6-star rating with both free and premium options available.
- [Tarotap](https://tarotap.com) - Modern AI tarot platform with clean interface and instant readings. No signup required, interpretations adapt to your situation.
- [Mystic AI Tarot](https://mysticaitarot.com) - AI-driven card interpretations that respond to your energy and questions.

## Interactive Tarot Reading Platforms

Platforms offering various spreads and interactive card selection experiences.

- [Trusted Tarot](https://trustedtarot.com) - One of the most established free tarot sites with over 20 years of experience. Features real shuffled cards uploaded daily and comprehensive card interpretations. 4.9-star rating from 66,000+ reviews.
- [Free-Tarot-Reading.net](https://free-tarot-reading.net) - Veteran platform with over 200 million readings delivered since 2002. Simple, no-frills approach to quick tarot guidance.
- [EvaTarot](https://evatarot.net) - Celtic Cross and Major Arcana readings based on traditional Rider-Waite deck. Features Eva Delattre as virtual ambassador guiding interpretations.
- [iFate](https://ifate.com) - Comprehensive divination platform offering tarot, I-Ching, runes, numerology, and biorhythms. Award-winning Celtic Cross spread available.
- [Facade Tarot](https://facade.com/tarot) - Classic internet tarot platform with straightforward readings and no signup required. Perfect for quick daily draws.
- [Lotus Tarot](https://lotustarot.com) - Specializes in the comprehensive Celtic Cross spread with thoughtful positional interpretations.
- [TellMyTarot](https://tellmytarot.com) - Specialized spreads for love, career, and general guidance. Features popular Love Tarot and Yes/No readings.

## Tarot Learning & Education

Resources for learning to read tarot cards and understanding card meanings.

- [Labyrinthos Academy](https://labyrinthos.co) - Online tarot school bringing ancient rituals to modern practice. Offers interactive readings, tutorials, and beautifully designed digital decks. Free lessons covering keywords, suit correspondences, and numerology basics.
- [Biddy Tarot](https://biddytarot.com) - Premier tarot education resource founded by Brigit Esselmont. Offers comprehensive courses, free webinars, community app, and the Biddy Tarot Podcast. Great for both beginners and advanced readers.
- [Golden Thread Tarot](https://goldenthreadtarot.com) - Modern minimalist approach to tarot education with beautiful app and physical deck. Focus on self-knowledge rather than fortune-telling.
- [Learn Tarot](https://learntarot.com) - Free comprehensive course covering all 78 cards with detailed symbolism explanations.

Platforms connecting you with professional tarot readers for personalized sessions.

- [Purple Garden](https://purplegarden.co) - Transparent review system with numerical breakdown of positive/negative feedback. Features video calls, live chat, and phone readings. Over 100 tarot specialists available.
- [Keen](https://keen.com) - Operating since 1999 with thousands of vetted psychic advisors. Sophisticated search filters, satisfaction guarantee, and mobile app available.
- [Kasamba](https://kasamba.com) - Global community since 2001 with millions of reviews. 200+ experienced tarot readers covering relationships, career, and personal growth.
- [California Psychics](https://californiapsychics.com) - 25+ years of experience with affordable readings starting at $1/minute. Educational tarot section and three-tier pricing packages.
- [Psychic Source](https://psychicsource.com) - Wide selection of live chat, phone, and video reading options. Known for experienced readers with decades of practice.
- [PathForward](https://pathforward.com) - Intimate pool of carefully selected tarot readers. Straightforward selection process and personalized sessions.
- [Mysticsense](https://mysticsense.com) - Rigorously screened advisors specializing in detailed tarot sessions. Focus on clear guidance for relationships, career, and personal growth.
